Privacy Policy for MedDay Flow

Last updated: September 2026

Dart Nexus Lab provides MedDay Flow as a utility application. This page informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data.

1. Information Collection & Usage

MedDay Flow allows you to manage daily schedules, tasks, and medication reminders. All task titles, routines, prescription names, dosages, and intake schedules are stored locally on your device for your personal productivity and health organization.

2. Biometric Authentication

If you enable biometric login (such as Face ID or fingerprint unlock), all biometric verification is handled strictly by your device's native operating system hardware. MedDay Flow never stores, transmits, or has access to your raw biometric data.

3. Notifications & Device Permissions

The app requests notification permissions solely to deliver scheduled reminders for your daily tasks and medications at your designated times.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal schedules, medication logs, or productivity data with any third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this policy, please contact Dart Nexus Lab through your registered developer contact email.
